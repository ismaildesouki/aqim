Privacy Policy for Aqim

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Aqim is a prayer companion app. It is designed so that your data stays on your device. This policy describes exactly what the app does with information, including the few cases where a request leaves your phone.

Location

  • Aqim asks for location access (while the app is in use) to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are.
  • Prayer times are calculated on your device. Your coordinates are not sent to any server for that purpose.
  • The last location prayer times were computed for is stored on your device so the app can show times immediately on the next launch.
  • If you prefer, you can turn location access off and choose a city manually in Settings.

Requests that leave your device

Aqim has no servers of its own. It contacts third-party services only in these cases:

  • Manual city search. When you type a city name in Settings, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service to look up its coordinates. The chosen coordinates are then stored on your device and reused; no further requests are made for prayer times. Search data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Surah Al-Kahf translations and recitation audio. When you open a translation or play recitation in the Al-Kahf reader, the verse text or audio is fetched from the AlQuran Cloud API. Only the surah and verse numbers are sent.
  • Links you open. Hadith references can open sunnah.com in your browser if you choose to.

None of these requests include your name, account, contacts, or precise usage history. Aqim has no user accounts.

Stored on your device

  • Your settings, alarms, sunnah selections, adhkar progress, and Quran bookmarks are stored locally using standard iOS and Android app storage.
  • Backups are created only when you ask for one, and are shared through the system share sheet to a destination you choose. Local backup files are deleted with the app if you uninstall it.

What Aqim does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No collection of personal identification information.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alarms are scheduled locally on your device. On iOS 26 and later, wake-up alarms use Apple's AlarmKit framework, which requires the "schedule alarms" permission. No push notification service is used.

Children's privacy

Aqim does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
