Common questions

My prayer times look wrong

Open Settings and check two things: the location Aqim is using, and the calculation method. Different authorities publish different angles for Fajr and Isha, so switching to the method your local mosque follows usually resolves a difference of a few minutes. You can also set a manual per-prayer adjustment.

My alarm did not ring

Aqim schedules alarms locally, so it needs notification permission, and on iOS 26 and later it also needs the "schedule alarms and timers" permission that is requested the first time you create a wake-up alarm. Check both in the iOS Settings app under Aqim. Alarms are rescheduled whenever you open the app or change a setting, so opening Aqim once after a long gap restores the full window.

The Qibla compass will not settle

The compass needs location and motion access, and it needs calibrating away from metal and magnets. Move the phone in a figure eight if iOS asks you to calibrate.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Settings has a backup export that writes a file you can save or send anywhere, and a restore that reads it back. Backups stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Aqim stores lives on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no server-side account to close.
