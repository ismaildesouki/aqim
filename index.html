Aqim

A prayer companion that keeps your data on your phone. Accurate prayer times, alarms that actually wake you, and the daily sunnah in one place.

Prayer times, computed on your device

Aqim calculates the five daily prayers and sunrise on your phone using thirteen calculation methods, including Muslim World League, ISNA, the Egyptian General Authority, Umm Al-Qura, Diyanet and MUIS. There is no prayer-times API call and your coordinates are never uploaded, so the times are right even with no signal.

Alarms that wake you for prayer

  • Set an alarm for any prayer, before or after the adhan.
  • Adhan or system sound, a snooze limit, and a gentle fade-in.
  • On iOS 26 and later, wake-up alarms use Apple's AlarmKit, so they ring from the lock screen through silent mode and Focus.
  • Pre-adhan and suhoor reminders, plus morning and evening adhkar.

Qibla, dates and the daily sunnah

  • A Qibla compass that points to Makkah from anywhere.
  • Hijri dates with Ramadan, the white days, Arafah and Ashura surfaced on the day.
  • Surah Al-Kahf for Fridays, with bookmarks, translation and recitation.
  • A sunnah tracker for daily practices and adhkar.

Privacy first

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no data sold or shared. Prayer times are computed locally and your location never leaves the device. Read the full privacy policy.
